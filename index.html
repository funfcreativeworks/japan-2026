📖 Curated Story & Experience

Founded in 645 AD, Sensō-ji is Tokyo’s oldest and most revered Buddhist temple. Under twilight illumination, the day's buzzing crowds dissipate, casting a peaceful glow upon the massive five-story pagoda and vermilion timber eaves.
